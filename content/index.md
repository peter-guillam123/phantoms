---
summary: Ghost stories by ten forgotten writers of 1880 to 1925, and the lives behind them.
---

Between about 1880 and 1925 the magazines of Britain and Ireland printed ghost stories by the thousand. A handful of the authors are still read. Most were clerks, soldiers, schoolteachers, clergymen’s daughters and newspapermen who wrote in the evenings, were paid a guinea or two, and were forgotten.

Here are ten of them. They never met. They had nothing in common but an editor in Edinburgh, and the fact that each had worked out, from the place they came from and the life they led, a quite different idea of what a ghost is.

Their lives come first. The first of the stories are further down, and the rest will follow.
